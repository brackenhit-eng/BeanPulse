About BeanPulse

BeanPulse is a South African independent music project by Jannie Bean, blending healing lyrics, uplifting electronic sounds, Afrikaans-English storytelling, spiritual themes, and emotional transformation. The music is created to inspire hope, peace, motivation, and inner healing.
